Who is Dinesh Datlana? (परिचय)

Dinesh Datlana (officially known as Dinesh Kashyap) is an artist, content creator, and tech enthusiast based in Datalana Kham, Kasganj, Uttar Pradesh. He runs the popular art page and channel "Dinesh Art Datlana" on YouTube and Instagram (@dinesh_art_datlana), where he shares realistic pencil and charcoal sketches. Alongside art, he works closely with electronics, circuit boards, and practical science models.

Key Interests & Projects (हुनर और काम)
